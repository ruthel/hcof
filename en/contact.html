Frequently asked questions

HCOF vs Hope's City: what is the difference?

HCOF is the organization that acts. Hope's City is the long-term vision, not yet built.

Is HCOF already active?

The association is being registered, with a founding in 2027. The first actions (food and school support) start with it.

Does HCOF house children?

Not in Phase 1. Residential care is planned for a later stage, under the supervision of the competent authorities.

Is HCOF a Christian association?

Yes. Our work is founded on the Word of God, and we serve every person without distinction of ethnicity, region or social status.

How will my gift be used?

It funds field actions. A receipt and a use-of-funds report are sent for any significant gift. See the Give page.

Who leads HCOF?

A three-member management committee (president, secretary general, treasurer) and a six-member general assembly. See Governance.
