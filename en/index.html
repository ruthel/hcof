Non-profit association being registered in Douala, Cameroon. Founded 2027.

Every child deserves a future.

Hope's City Outreach Foundation (HCOF) supports orphans, widows and the most vulnerable in Cameroon through concrete action and transparent management.

What we do today

HCOF starts in 2027 with simple, achievable actions: feed, support schooling, relieve hardship.

Phase 1 · 2027

Food support

Gifts of food and essential goods to orphanages, homeless people and struggling families.

Phase 1 · 2027

School support

Help with schooling for vulnerable children and teenagers, with priority up to age 15.

Phase 1 · 2027

Transparency

A receipt and a use-of-funds report for every significant gift, plus an annual financial report.
