Our journey

Four stages, from today's fieldwork to tomorrow's foundation.

HCOF was designed to evolve. Each stage widens our means and our responsibilities, and each depends on the results of the one before.

  1. Phase 1 · 2027

    1. Non-profit association, 2027

    Registration in Douala, setting up the management committee, first actions: food and school support. First public report.

  2. Goal

    2. NGO

    Expanded programs (training, health), international partnerships, first institutional funding.

  3. Goal

    3. Public-utility association

    Official recognition of demonstrated work. Residential care for children, support for widows, family reintegration.

  4. Vision

    4. Foundation

    The name “Foundation” is adopted at the statutory transformation: Hope's City Foundation, international reach and the realization of Hope's City.

Our commitment to clarity

Dates for stages 2 to 4 are not set. This site always states what exists, what is in preparation and what remains a vision.
