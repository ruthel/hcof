Get involved

There is a place for everyone, on site in Douala or remotely.

HCOF starts in 2027. The first people who join us help lay good foundations. Choose the way that suits you.

Become a volunteer

Offer time or a skill, on site in Douala or remotely.

  • School support and student mentoring
  • Preparing and handing out gifts
  • Communication, graphic design, photography
  • French / English translation
  • Accounting, management, law
  • Listening, visits, prayer
Offer my help

Become a partner

Companies, institutions, schools, orphanages and humanitarian organizations: let's build measurable actions, with a report at the end.

  • Gifts of food, supplies or services
  • Funding of a specific action
  • Sharing of skills
  • Introductions to other stakeholders

Churches and ministries

Join your community to our mission among orphans and widows: prayer, collections, visits, relaying our needs.

Support an action

Choose to fund a specific action, such as a food basket or a school start-up kit. You will receive a report on how it was used.

How it works

  1. You write to us

    Tell us who you are and what you can offer.

  2. We talk

    A first conversation lets us define together a contribution that fits your availability and our needs.

  3. We commit to each other

    Anyone in contact with children commits to our child protection policy.

  4. You act, we report

    We document every action and keep you informed of its results.

Spread the vision

Talking about HCOF around you is already valuable help. Share this site, introduce us to your church, school or company. Journalists and ambassadors will find our logos and presentation texts in the media kit.
