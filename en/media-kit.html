About in brief

Short version (50 words)

Hope's City Outreach Foundation (HCOF) is a Christian non-profit association based in Douala, Cameroon. It supports orphans, vulnerable children, widows and homeless people, starting with food and school support, under transparent management.

Long version

Born from a calling in 2021, HCOF is established in Douala in 2027 under Cameroonian law no. 90/053 on freedom of association. Its mission: to restore to the least privileged the basic needs, spiritual, social and psychological, that they require. It moves step by step, from association to foundation, and always distinguishes what exists from what remains a vision, such as the Hope's City project. Founder: Landry MBOM.

Fact sheet

  • Name: Hope's City Outreach Foundation (HCOF)
  • Status: non-profit association, law no. 90/053 of 19 December 1990 (Cameroon)
  • Head office: Douala, Littoral region, Cameroon
  • Founder: Landry MBOM, Vision Bearer
  • Founded: 2027, following a calling received in 2021
  • Languages: French and English
  • Signature: Hope. Protection. Transformation.
  • Motto: “As long as God reigns, everything can change.”
