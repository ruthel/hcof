Hope's City

The long-term vision. It is not yet built.

Vision

A place to restore lives

Hope's City is HCOF's long-term project: a city of living, learning and mutual support, designed to embody our vision. It does not exist yet. HCOF is its first step, and each milestone will be announced here only once it is truly under way.

Vision

Learn

Education and vocational training.

Vision

Heal

Access to healthcare.

Vision

Produce

Agriculture and income-generating activities.

Vision

Live together

A place of welcome and community mutual aid.

Where do we stand?

The project is at the vision stage. It will take shape at the “Foundation” stage of our journey. No land or building is announced to date.
