Where your gift goes

In Phase 1, gifts directly fund field actions: food, supplies and school fees.

Financial accountability

HCOF commits to:

  • issue a receipt for any significant gift;
  • send the donor a use-of-funds report;
  • hold funds in bank accounts in the association's name, with joint signature of the president and the treasurer;
  • close its accounts every 31 December and present an annual financial report;
  • be open to an external audit.
